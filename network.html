Why a Network

More than maintenance.

Most web designers offer "maintenance plans" — fix bugs, push updates, charge a fee. Functional, but transactional. The Soma Network is built different.

When you become a member, you join a community of business owners who are growing alongside you. You get ongoing care, yes — but also visibility, cross-promotion, and direct access to Soma's design and dev work. You become part of an ecosystem built on the same values that built your business: faith, integrity, and community.

Members rise together.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I have to be a Soma Industries client to join?

No, but having a website helps. If your site was built somewhere else, we can audit it and onboard you to the Network.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Membership is month-to-month. Cancel with 30 days notice — no contracts, no penalties, no guilt.

Do unused hours roll over?

Up to one month's worth of hours rolls over. After that, hours expire to keep things active and fair on both sides.

Can I upgrade or downgrade tiers?

Absolutely. Change anytime — upgrades take effect immediately, downgrades take effect the next billing cycle.

What if my needs exceed my tier's hours?

Extra hours billed at $40/hour for members (vs $50/hour for non-members) — or upgrade to the next tier. We'll tell you when you're approaching your limit.

Is there a Black-business priority?

Black-owned business members are prioritized in monthly spotlights and giveaway features. Mission alignment matters.
