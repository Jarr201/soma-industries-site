Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does a project take?

Brand identity projects typically take 1–3 weeks. Websites range from 2–6 weeks depending on scope. I'll give you a clear timeline at the start of every project.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes — 50% upfront, 50% on delivery. This keeps the process fair and ensures we're both committed to the outcome from day one.

Can I see examples of your work first?

Absolutely — reach out directly and I'll share examples relevant to your project.

Do you only work with Black-owned businesses?

Supporting Black small businesses is the core mission — but I'm open to any project that aligns with the values of community, integrity, and genuine impact.

What about Strong Stewards?

Strong Stewards is currently in active development. Use the contact form to join the interest list and be the first to know about launch dates and beta access.

Do you offer payment plans?

For larger projects, flexible payment structures are available. Reach out and we'll work out something that makes sense for your situation.
