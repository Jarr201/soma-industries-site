Our Mission

More Than Design.
It's a Movement.

I founded Soma Industries to help close the resource gap for Black-owned small businesses. Too many dreamers on the cusp of greatness lay their ideas to rest because they lack the ability to bring it to life.

Being stylish and God driven is behind everything we do. We bring together web development and content creation to give businesses the firepower they need to get more eyes on their products and services. All of our moves are grounded in the Christian values of excellence and stewardship. And as we grow, any entrepreneur or supporter that shares our values can, and should, join the family! The more the merrier! Let's build something unbreakable together.
