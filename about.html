About
Soma Industries

Who I Am

Hey, I'm Soma.

I'm a designer, developer, and entrepreneur looking to help businesses catch their footing and get a move on with a special focus on Black-owned small businesses, faith-driven brands, and other people who don't have access to traditional agency support.

I started Soma Industries LLC because I grew up knowing many business owners who had wonderful ideas without the expertise in design or planning. I simply don't belive that a business should be held back simply by lack of access to design direction or technical know=how. The goal is to help as many people that look and dream like me as I can.

My faith in God has always been super important to me and building towards a community of like minded people brings me a LOT of joy. God has a part in everything I touch and I plan on keeping it that way.

When I'm not designing or studying, you can find me on Twitch, and soon YouTube. I'm also deep in development on Strong Stewards, a Christian lifestyle app built to help believers lock in on their bodies and their faith in God together. Hoping to shoot it into the Google Play Store very soon!

The Vision

Where We're Going

I want Soma Industries to eventually grow into a full creative studio and a multimedia force of nature. I want to build resources and a community for black creatives and owners alike.

Alongside helping people bring their vision and dreams to life, content creation has always been a dream of mine and I'm thankful to finally be in a place that allows these things to be possibilities.
