About
Soma Industries

Who I Am

Hey, I'm Soma.

I'm a designer, developer, and entrepreneur on a mission to help Black-owned small businesses show up powerfully in a digital world. I started Soma Industries LLC because I believe that incredible products and services deserve incredible brands — and that no business should be held back simply by lack of access to design talent.

My faith is not a background detail — it's the foundation everything is built on. The belief that we're called to serve, to build, and to pour into the communities God has placed us in shapes every project I take on. That's not marketing. That's how I live.

When I'm not designing or building, you can find me on Twitch and YouTube — sharing knowledge, creating community, and talking honestly about what it takes to build something that lasts. I'm also deep in development on Strong Stewards, a Christian lifestyle app built to help believers steward their bodies and their faith together.

What Drives This Work

The Values Behind
Every Project

✝

Faith First

Every decision starts here. Not as a slogan — as a practice. Integrity, excellence, and genuine service are the natural output of a faith-centered approach to work.

✊

Community Investment

Soma Industries exists specifically to serve Black-owned businesses. This isn't a niche — it's a calling. We show up with full commitment to the community we're built for.

🎯

Intentional Design

Nothing is default, nothing is generic. Every visual choice has a reason. We design with purpose — brands that don't just look good, but work hard for their owners.

📣

Radical Transparency

Clear pricing, honest timelines, and direct communication. You always know what you're getting and why. No upsells you don't need. No confusion by design.

The Vision

Where We're Going

Soma Industries is growing into a full creative studio — a trusted resource for Black entrepreneurs who want world-class design without the world-class price tag of agencies that weren't built for them.

Alongside client work, content creation is a core part of the mission: educating entrepreneurs, building in public, and showing the community what's possible when faith and skillset meet.

And Strong Stewards — the app in development — is the beginning of a product side that reflects what we believe: that wholeness in body, mind, and spirit makes better builders.
